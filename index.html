OFFICIAL NOTICE BOARD

International Islamic University Chittagong

Department of Computer & Communication Engineering

Published Date: 27.09.26 | Schedule of Mid-Term Examination, Autumn 2026

All students of the Department of Computer & Communication Engineering (CCE) are hereby notified that the Mid-Term Examination for Autumn 2026 will commence on 30th September 2026 (Wednesday) and conclude on 14th October 2026 (Wednesday) according to the published schedule.

Important Examination Rules:

  • Students must bring their official Student ID card and Admit Card to the exam hall.
  • 1st Slot Exam: 09:30 AM – 11:00 AM (Entry allowed until 09:40 AM).
  • 2nd Slot Exam: 02:30 PM – 04:00 PM (Entry allowed until 02:40 PM).
  • Mobile phones, smartwatches, and programmable devices are strictly prohibited inside the hall.
